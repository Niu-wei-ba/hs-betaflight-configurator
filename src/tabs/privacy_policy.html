Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 5, 2026

This mirror site operates an independent Betaflight-compatible configurator deployment. This privacy policy explains what data is stored locally in your browser, what requests are made to mirror services, and what is not collected.

What We Store Locally

The configurator stores preferences and working state in your browser, including UI settings, recently used options, cached firmware metadata, preset source configuration, and similar operational data required for the application to function.

Device Access

The configurator may request access to serial, USB, Bluetooth, or other device interfaces when you choose to connect to a flight controller. These permissions are handled by your browser or operating system and are used only to communicate with devices you explicitly select.

Network Requests

The configurator may send requests to mirror-controlled services for the following purposes:

  • Loading firmware targets, releases, build options, and build status
  • Downloading firmware files
  • Loading preset indexes and preset content from configured preset sources
  • Opening documentation links that you choose to visit

No Third-Party Analytics

This mirror deployment does not include third-party analytics, sponsor delivery, advertising trackers, account login, or cloud backup features.

Third-Party Links

If you open links to source hosting, documentation, or user-configured preset repositories, those destinations operate under their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Browser-stored data remains on your device until it expires, is overwritten, or you clear site data manually. Mirror servers may retain normal access logs according to their own operational policies.

Security

No Internet transmission or local storage method is completely secure. You should review your browser permissions, keep local devices trusted, and validate firmware and preset sources before use.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated when mirror behavior changes. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.

Contact

For questions about this mirror deployment, use the project repository or mirror operator contact channel.
